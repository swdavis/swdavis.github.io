A list of some of the noteworthy events and honors for the group over the last several years:

November 2026: Shane Davis is named a University of Virginia College of Arts & Sciences Dean's Research Fellow.

October 2026: Katie Porter joins UVA Astronomy as a VITA Fellow.

November 2025: We (PI Davis) are awarded a DOE INCITE allocation of more than a million GPU node hours on Frontier and Aurora to simulate tidal disruption events.

August 2025: Tegan Thomas joins the group.

August 2025: Dr. Connor McClellan defends his PhD thesis.

March 2025: Connor McClellan accepts a postdoctoral position at Michigan State to work with the TARDIS collaboration.

October 2024: Dr. Sergei Dyda begins his new postdoctoral research position at the University of Alabama.

August 2023: Maria Renee Meza joins the group.

June 2023: Dr. Xiaoshan Huang defends her PhD thesis.

May 2023: Dr. Bri Mills defends their PhD thesis.

January 2023: Xiaoshan Huang accepts a postdoctoral fellowship at Caltech's Burke Institute.

October 2021: As part of the Disks and Winds Network, our proposal is selected by NASA for a highly competitive Theoretical and Computational Astrophysics Network award.

October 2021: Dr. Sergei Dyda joins the group as a postdoctoral researcher.

March 2021: Connor McClellan receives an honorable mention from the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program.

June 2020: Bri Mills is selected as a Jefferson Fellow.

June 2020: Xiaoshan Huang is selected for a pre-doctoral fellowship at the Flatiron Institute's Center for Computational Astrophysics.

April 2019: Shane Davis is selected for an All University Teaching Award.
